Separate complete conflict alternatives from the editable whole-task resolution, with responsive comparison cards, selected-state cues, and save-state input protection.

When a task has conflicting versions, you can compare every version side by side or in a stacked view on a narrow screen. The selected version and your editable resolution are clearly labeled, and your edits remain available if saving fails.
