Add the AnyDash application hosts on the shared ReactLynx client: the Lynx for Web browser host with its preview and axe checks, the Lynxtron desktop host with the embedded collector, tray residency, deep links, login item and signed-feed updates, the iOS and Android Lynx hosts, and native widgets for iOS (WidgetKit), Android (App Widget), macOS (separate WidgetKit host) and the Windows Widgets Board (separate MSIX provider). Register the `anydash` Portless route, the affected CI job and the public build variables.

<!-- public-changelog:start -->

AnyDash is available on the web, on macOS and Windows, and on iOS and Android. One dashboard shows your AI coding subscription limits, local AI session usage from your devices, and deployment platform status, usage and cost. You can share selected widgets with your team without sharing your connections, and add an AnyDash widget to your home screen, desktop or Widgets Board.
